# Wallet Pickle protected terms

The house style and protected-terms list for Wallet Pickle content. It extends
`../SKILL.md` and `wallet-pickle-voice.md`. Add a row whenever the voice layer keeps
rewriting a term in a way that loses precision or breaks house style. Keep this list
small; a long list nobody maintains is worse than a short one that is current.

## How to use this file

- A **protected term** must not be casually replaced with a looser synonym, even
  when the voice layer would normally vary word choice.
- A **preferred term** is the term to use when more than one common word means the
  same thing.
- **Regulatory and disclosure language** must never be rewritten for personality;
  see "Regulatory and disclosure text" below.

## Preferred terminology

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| interest percentage | interest rate |
| credit history | credit score, or name the specific report or score |

## Protected financial terms

Keep these precise. Do not swap them for a softer or vaguer synonym just to vary
the prose.

| Term | Why it is protected |
|---|---|
| APR | A specific rate, distinct from a plain interest rate. Do not blur the two. |
| deductible | A specific insurance term. Do not replace with "fee" or "cost." |
| premium | A specific insurance term. Do not replace with "payment" or "price" when the source means the recurring premium. |
| FICO score / credit score | Do not use interchangeably with "credit rating" unless the source does. |

## Product and brand names

Add Wallet Pickle's own product and feature names here as they are introduced, with
the exact capitalization to use. None are defined yet; add rows here instead of
inventing names.

## Regulatory and disclosure text

Treat legal, regulatory, advertising, affiliate, and insurance disclosures as
protected content. Do not rewrite them for personality, tone, or brevity unless the
user explicitly asks. Preserve every link and every required disclosure exactly as
given.
