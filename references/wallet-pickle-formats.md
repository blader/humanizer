# Wallet Pickle formats

This file extends `../SKILL.md` and `wallet-pickle-voice.md`. Use it when the user
names one of the formats below. Each entry describes what the format needs, not a
template to fill in; see "Avoid template writing" in `wallet-pickle-voice.md`.

## Articles

Useful, specific, and readable. Write toward search intent without sounding like it
was written for search. Lead with the most useful or surprising supported fact.

## Newsletters

More personality and faster pacing than an article. Open with a real hook, keep it
skimmable, and end with a takeaway the reader can use, not a summary of what they
just read.

## Quizzes

Playful, concise, and accurate. Covers personality quizzes, money-habit quizzes,
insurance quizzes, financial literacy quizzes, "which type of spender are you,"
"can you guess," and other consumer quizzes. Every question and result must be
genuinely fun to read, not just structurally a quiz.

## Trivia

Surprising, accurate, and concise. Cut a fact that is technically true but not
actually interesting.

## Polls

Word the question to invite a genuine opinion or reaction, not a leading or obvious
answer.

## Puzzles

Covers logic puzzles, money puzzles, financial math, "spot the mistake," decision
puzzles, and consumer riddles. The math and the facts must check out; a puzzle that
is wrong is worse than a puzzle that is easy.

## Crosswords

Clues must be clever, accurate, and tied to the piece's theme.

## Listicles

Each item must be specific and editorially useful on its own, not a restatement of
the intro with a number in front of it.

## Social

Covers Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X/Threads-style posts, and
Reddit-native discussion prompts. Write native to the platform: do not paste an
article and shorten it. Match the platform's real conventions (caption length, hook
style, whether a question invites real discussion) and keep every claim as
supported as the source article.
